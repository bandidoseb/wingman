**↓ Dig card next evolution** — design conversation captured 2026-07-11, NOT built. Full note:
`Wingman Brain/_Inbox/2026-07-11 Dig card — threading, exhaustion, and natural transition.md` (read it
in full before starting the session).

Key decisions locked in that conversation:
- **Do NOT split the down arrow** into a breadth/depth sub-menu — fights "bumpers not a script / never
  robotic", adds mid-call friction, collides with the ← answer / → close compass. The single ↓ already
  produces "best next question" from belief ladder + last ~120s; breadth-vs-depth is emergent, not a fork.
- **Real bug found:** priority order aims ⬜ open (cold) belief AHEAD of 🟡 partial (half-said), so a press
  meant to deepen a live thread can jump to a cold belief.
- **Solve = threading (no menu):** repeat ↓ press on a live thread = chunk down on their fresh answer;
  ~2-min flow window mirroring the objection flow.
- **Exhaustion detection needs TWO signals:** belief ✅ locked (the "dug enough" signal) BUT the ladder
  re-scores only ~every 2 min, so it lags — pair it with the flow's own recent back-and-forth.
- **Hardest part = the override path** when Wingman guesses deeper-vs-switch wrong; default to STAYING on
  the thread, switch only on a clear lock, and NEVER switch silently.
- **Show the belief on the card = ESSENTIAL** (DIG · PAIN → DIG · DESIRE is the rep's cue the topic moved).
  Already half-built (belief tag resolves pre-paint; objection flow has MOVE #N chrome).
- **THE deep open problem — natural transition:** "weakest belief" = most IMPORTANT, not most NATURAL.
  Pure importance = the robotic failure. Natural = the next belief is reachable by a BRIDGE from what they
  just said; decide on importance × reachability, and flow wins on any single question. Emotional momentum
  governs stay-vs-move (after a vulnerable disclosure, STAY and deepen). Seeds already exist (the dig
  prompt's ORDER doctrine is the natural emotional arc; "anchor to their exact words" bridges phrasing) —
  but belief SELECTION (weakest link) can override flow; the build must reconcile them.

**Precondition:** live-test the current single-shot dig (+ Phase 1b Sales DNA) on real calls FIRST; design
threading against real misses, not in the abstract. Related: [[project-offer-aware-moat]].

**Concrete #6 bug (voice grind 2026-07-19):** the single-shot dig `support` + `money-qualifier` fixtures produce NON-SEQUITURS — the prompt probes whichever belief is ⬜ open on the ladder while IGNORING what the prospect just said (Sebastian, twice: "seems random"). This IS the belief-SELECTION-by-natural-transition gap above, made concrete: the fix must anchor the dig to the LATEST utterance (bridge from what they said) or stay silent on a dead-end statement, NOT jump to an unrelated open belief. Also flagged: money-qualifier NAMING — the card asks how much they WANT to earn, but "qualifier" implies probing capital they HAVE; reconcile the label when building. Contained, own session.
