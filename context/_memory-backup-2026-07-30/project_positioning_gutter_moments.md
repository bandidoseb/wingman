Wingman's sharpest positioning, which Sebastian flagged for the website + marketing (Track 2):

**The pitch:** "A rep who needs Wingman to tell them how to start a discovery call has a bigger problem than Wingman can fix." Wingman isn't for the opening — it's for the gutter moments:
- **Minute 18** — the prospect gives a vague answer and the rep accepts it instead of chunking down
- **Minute 34** — the real objection shows up wearing a costume
- **Minute 41** — they've built pain and desire and don't know they're allowed to close

**Why:** it converts product rules #10/#11 (bumpers not a script; never make reps robots) into a *sales* argument. Handing a confident closer a generic opener is worse than handing them nothing — it makes them sound scripted at the exact moment the prospect forms a first impression. This is also the honest answer to "why doesn't it talk more?", which is the #1 objection a skeptical closer will raise.

**How to apply:** use as the website's core narrative + the Test Pilot pitch. It doubles as the product's design north star — the arrows earn their keep mid-call, not at the open. Pairs with [[project-positioning-wedge]] (generic vs offer-aware side-by-side). Drove the [[project-card-voice-rework]] cold-open ruling: when the prospect hasn't said anything substantive, Wingman admits it has nothing rather than fabricating an opener.
