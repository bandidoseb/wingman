# EXPECTED — Belief Ladder summarizer answer key

Pre-registered **before** running the harness, so grading is objective (catch the planted trap) instead of vibes. Each transcript plants one known failure mode. **Pass = the summarizer catches the trap. Fail = it gets fooled.**

Belief statuses: ✅ locked · 🟡 partial · ⬜ open · ⚠️ at-risk/objection.

---

## 01-fake-yes.txt — Enthusiasm ≠ commitment
**Offer:** DFY Meta ads agency, $8k/mo. **Prospect:** Jordan (supplement brand).
**Trap:** Jordan gushes ("sounds amazing," "exactly what we need") but never commits — budget is "that could work I think," partner **Dev** controls the money, timeline is vague ("soon"), ends "let me circle back."
**Correct grading:**
- **Money → ⬜ open / ⚠️ at-risk** — "could work I think" is NOT confirmation. **Never ✅.**
- **Support → ⚠️ at-risk** — partner Dev controls the money / must approve.
- Desire → 🟡 at best ("200k, who wouldn't" is not an owned goal).
- Pain → 🟡/✅ (he does own a messy current state: media buyer quit, "it's a mess").
- **Close-readiness → NOT green** (too early).
- **Weakest link → Money + Support.**
**FAIL IF:** any belief marked ✅ off enthusiasm · Money ✅ · close-readiness green.

## 02-guarded-skeptic.txt — Rep-surfaced facts ≠ prospect-owned pain
**Offer:** Health/fitness coaching, $5k. **Prospect:** Ray (defensive, minimizes).
**Trap:** Real pain leaks (A1C pre-diabetic, dad's heart attack at 55, off bloodwork) but Ray **downplays every single one** ("tons of people are," "I'm not him," "it's manageable"). The facts are alarming; the prospect has NOT owned them.
**Correct grading:**
- **Pain → 🟡 partial (NOT ✅)** — the rep surfaced the facts; Ray actively minimizes. Owning the facts ≠ owning the pain.
- **Doubt (can't DIY) → ⚠️** — "most of that stuff is a scam," "I'll just cut some carbs."
- **Money → ⚠️** — "$5k for what exactly."
- **Trust → ⚠️** — guarded, "scam," "send me info."
- Desire → ⬜ (no desired outcome stated).
- **Close-readiness → red/early.** **Weakest link → Pain ownership + Trust.**
**FAIL IF:** Pain ✅ because the bloodwork/family-history facts are scary · close-readiness anything but early.

## 03-strong-close.txt — REVERSE trap: a real close (don't be falsely cautious)
**Offer:** Sales coaching, $12k. **Prospect:** Tasha (referral, fully bought-in).
**Trap (reverse):** Everything genuinely LOCKS — the AI must recognize it and go mostly ✅ / green. Being over-cautious here (marking owned beliefs 🟡) is the failure.
**Correct grading:**
- **Pain ✅** ("leaving money on the table, embarrassing, eating at me," 18% close rate).
- **Cost ✅** ("$8-10k a month on the floor, a house payment").
- **Desire ✅** ("$30k months," own words) · why = buy mom a house cash.
- **Money ✅** ("already set it aside, sitting there ready").
- **Support ✅** ("all me, my own decisions").
- **Trust ✅** ("Marcus went from where I am to $40k, seen it with my own eyes, trust the process").
- **Close-readiness → GREEN. Weakest link → "all locked — ready to close."**
**FAIL IF:** owned beliefs marked 🟡/⬜ (false caution) · close-readiness not green.

## 04-contradiction-longcall.txt — Recent-overrides-stale + long-call memory
**Offer:** E-commerce growth consulting, $18k. **Prospect:** Priya.
**Trap (two-part):**
1. **Money updates:** early she says budget is "$2-3k"; later, as pain deepens, "I'd find $15-20k... we have it." The summary's Money line must reflect the **updated ~$18-20k willingness**, NOT the stale "$2-3k."
2. **Long-call memory:** in **line 2** she says "my husband and I run this together"; the husband isn't mentioned again until the **final** exchange ("wouldn't sign $18k without him"). The summary must still carry **husband = co-decision-maker** in Support at the end (it survives ~5 chunks of no mention).
**Correct grading:**
- **Money → reflects ~$18-20k / "would invest if it works"** (NOT "$2-3k"). Recent overrides stale.
- **Support → ⚠️ at-risk** — husband co-decides, must sign off on $18k.
- Pain ✅ ("would crush us," "can't let it die"). Cost ✅ ("this year").
- **Close-readiness → not green** (husband gate). **Weakest link → husband sign-off (Support).**
**FAIL IF:** Money still says "$2-3k" (stale) · husband dropped from Support by the end (memory loss).

## 05-hidden-objection.txt — The real objection is hidden behind logistics
**Offer:** Coaching for course creators, $9k. **Prospect:** Kevin.
**Trap:** Kevin buries the room in logistics questions (calls/week, recorded?, group size, results timeline, refund policy) that look like buying questions but are stalling. The REAL objection, surfaced late, is **fear of failing again / self-doubt** ("I bought a course and never finished... if I drop nine grand and still don't follow through, then it's me, I'm the problem"). He explicitly says **money is fine** ("I have it set aside, that's not the issue").
**Correct grading:**
- **Money → ✅** (explicitly set aside, "not the issue"). **Must NOT be flagged as the objection.**
- **Open objection / Weakest link → fear of failure / self-trust / follow-through** — NOT logistics, NOT money, NOT the refund policy.
- Pain → 🟡/✅ (wants to scale $4k → six figures). Trust → 🟡.
**FAIL IF:** the objection is listed as money / price / refund / logistics · the self-doubt/fear is missed as the weakest link.
*(This is the judgment test — the likeliest place Sonnet separates from Haiku.)*
