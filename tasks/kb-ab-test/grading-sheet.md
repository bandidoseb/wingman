# KB A/B — Blind Grading Sheet

For each objection you'll see the call setup, then **4 cards in random order** (no labels).
Pick the **single best card** — the one you'd most want on screen at that moment.

**How to answer:** reply with one line per objection, e.g. `1:3` (objection 1, card 3).
All on one line is fine: `1:3 2:1 3:4 ...`. If two are basically tied, pick either. If all 4 are bad, write `x`.

Don't overthink it — go with your gut as a closer. ~15 min.
