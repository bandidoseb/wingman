# New Objection Prompt — results vs the old cards

New card = one first-person line the rep says (Cole/Ryder framework + belief-ladder context).

### #1 — "I need to think about it"

**Discovery (context fed in):**
> Prospect: Jenna, solo bookkeeper, ~$6k/mo, turning away clients because she's maxed on hours.
> Pain uncovered: no systems to delegate; tried one contractor, micromanaged it, gave up.
> Money: she has the $9k saved — said plainly "money's not really the issue."
> Decision: solo, entirely her call.
> Emotion: comfortable but stuck; scared to take her hands off the wheel.
> Wants: $20k/mo and an actual team.
> Weakest link: she believes it would work and can afford it — the stall is fear of change, not money or logistics.

**🟢 NEW (framework):** Yeah, totally — and I hear that. But be honest with me, Jenna — is it that you need more information, or is it that handing this off just scares you a little?

<sub>old free (no KB): What specifically are you worried will happen? — I'm sensing this isn't about the program itself — you said it sounds right. What's the real concern you're sitting with?</sub>  
<sub>old KB: What specifically would you think about? — Is it the delegation part, the investment, or something else — because we can address it right now.</sub>

### #2 — "It's too expensive"

**Discovery (context fed in):**

> Pain uncovered: 70-hour weeks; every job needs him; no time to grow.
> Money: flinched at $9k — but mentioned they just put $15k into new equipment.
> Decision: his call.
> Emotion: proud, frustrated, a little skeptical of "coaching."
> Wants: step back, $50k/mo, a manager running jobs.
> Weakest link: he's framing $9k as an expense, not ROI — hasn't connected it to the cost of two more years stuck at 70-hour weeks.

**🟢 NEW (framework):** Yeah, I get it — $9k hits different when you're not expecting it. But Andre, can I ask you something real quick — two more years at 70-hour weeks, what's that actually costing you?

<sub>old free (no KB): What's two more years costing you? — You just invested $15k in equipment to grow — what's staying stuck at 70 hours a week actually running you in lost time and money over the next 24 months?</sub>  
<sub>old KB: Flip the math on him. — Andre, what's the real cost of staying exactly where you are for two more years — 70-hour weeks, every job depending on you, no manager, stuck at $30k?</sub>
