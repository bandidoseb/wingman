Full Design Spec — Scientifically Sourced
Position
Bottom-left
80px from bottom, 32px from left edge
Z-pattern + gaze aversion research, EBU subtitle standards
Animation In
Fade in place
200ms, ease-in curve. No slide, no scale, no bounce.
Franconeri & Simons 2003, Google Glass HIG, FAA HUD standard
Animation Out
Fade out
150ms ease-out. Abrupt disappearance also triggers orienting reflex.
Yantis & Jonides 1984 — onset/offset attention capture
Background
rgba(18,18,24, 0.82)
Dark blue-gray, 82% opacity + backdrop blur
Bauer & Cavonius 1980, automotive HUD opacity research
Headline Text
18px, weight 700
rgba(255,255,255,0.93) — near-white, not pure white
Arditi & Cho 2005 — peripheral font size requirements
Support Text
12px, weight 400
rgba(255,255,255,0.42) — clearly secondary, italic
Visual hierarchy — subordinate text shouldn't compete with headline
Font
System sans-serif
SF Pro on Mac. No dyslexic font — benefit is dyslexia-specific, not general.
Arditi & Cho 2005 — sans-serif superior for peripheral readability
Card Width
280px max
Nudge cards ~220px. Objection cards up to 280px.
NHTSA glance research — minimize eye travel from primary task
Word Count
Headline: ≤5 words
Body: 1 sentence max. Headline alone must be actionable.
Pashler 1994 dual-task + Arnsten 2009 stress/WM research
Ambient Element
Always-on dot
6px dot at bottom-left. Always visible. Card expands from it.
Stothart et al. 2015 — eliminates on-call attention cost
Accent Colors
None / muted only
No red, green, orange, blue text. Saturated colors activate focal attention.
Treisman Feature Integration Theory — color as pre-attentive feature
Border
rgba(255,255,255,0.09)
Barely visible. Defines the card without demanding attention.
Ambient/focal attention model — secondary info should be detectable, not demanding

Card Format by Stage
Rapport → Discovery → Transition: Headline only. ≤4 words. Pure directional nudge. No body text.
Objections → Close: Headline (≤5 words, actionable alone) + italic support sentence (example question or framing). Rep reads headline on glance, body during natural pause before responding.
