# Wingman Card Voice Spec — the anti-AI tonality bar

Source: Sebastian's line-by-line feedback on the confrontation batch (2026-07-16). Sebastian is the
FINAL tonality judge; the AI judge scored these tics 4.8/5 and could not feel the AI-ness. This
spec is the human bar. His rewrites ARE the target voice — use them as few-shot examples.

North star: a card so natural that Cole Gordon reads it and thinks "this is legit," and a brand-new
closer reads it and it flows out of their mouth like their own words. If a rep would never actually
say it out loud, it's wrong — no matter how "correct" the strategy is.

---

## BANNED — these instantly read as AI (hard-fail, never ship)
- **"can I ask" / "can I ask you something" / "can I ask you something honestly?"** — nobody talks
  like this. It also creates the awkward permission-ask-then-wait problem (does the rep pause for a
  yes, or bulldoze past it?). KILL IT. Flow straight into the question instead.
- **"come on"** (as a lead-in) — the challenger says it constantly. Sounds like an AI chatbot.
  Strip it; the line is stronger without it. "What did that coach actually not do?" > "Come on, what
  did that coach actually not do?"
- **Forced empathy clichés** — "getting burned is real", "that feeling makes total sense",
  "that nervousness is actually a good sign". Canned AI attempts to relate to a human emotion. Cut or
  replace with something a real person would actually say.
- Any acknowledgment that names/narrates the emotion instead of just talking like a human.

## THE VOICE — acknowledge briefly, then FLOW straight into the question (one breath)
Natural connectors Sebastian actually uses (rotate — don't overuse any one):
- "honestly," / "but honestly," / "I'm curious," / "I'm curious though," / "question —" /
  "I gotcha," / "for sure," / "yeah, totally," / "I hear you, but honestly,"
The acknowledgment and the question are ONE flowing thought, not a permission-ask + a pause + a
question. Warm, human, conversational — never a script, never an email, never a chatbot.

## GOLD — Sebastian's own rewrites (the target voice; use as few-shot anchors)

| Situation | AI version (bad) | Sebastian's human version (target) |
|---|---|---|
| trust-burned · challenger | "Come on — what did that coach actually not do? Because until I know that, 'different' doesn't mean anything." | "What did that coach actually not do? Because until I know that, 'different' doesn't mean anything." (strip "come on") |
| timing · challenger | "Come on — be straight with me. What's actually making next quarter better than now?" | "I hear you, but honestly — what's actually making next quarter better than now?" |
| timing · balanced | "Yeah, totally. Can I ask you something honestly? Is it more that the timing genuinely isn't right, or you're not fully sure this'll work for you?" | "Yeah, totally. Honestly — is it more that the timing genuinely isn't right, or are you not fully sure this'll actually work for you?" |
| send-info · consultative | "Yeah, of course — can I ask, is there something you'd want to feel more sure about before saying yes?" | "Yeah, for sure, I can send some info over — but I'm curious, is there something you'd want to feel more sure about before saying yes?" |
| send-info · balanced | "Yeah, totally — before I do that, can I ask you something? Is it more that you need time to think, or is there a part of this you're not fully sure about?" | "Yeah, totally, but I'm curious — before I do that: is it more time you need to think, or is there a part of this you're not fully sure about yet?" |
| diy · direct | "I hear you — and you probably could. But you've been grinding on this for 18 months already. So what's different this time?" | "Yeah, I hear you, and you probably could — but you've been grinding on this for 18 months already. What would be different moving forward if you did it on your own?" |
| competitor · consultative | "Yeah, totally — and can I ask you something? What would you want to feel sure about before this becomes a clear yes for you?" | "Okay, I gotcha. I'm curious though — what would you want to actually feel sure about before this becomes a clear yes for you?" |

Also flagged as nonsense (fix the base prompt, not just the tic): diy · consultative "what's made the
last eighteen months feel different from this time?" — doesn't parse. Cards must always make literal
sense.
