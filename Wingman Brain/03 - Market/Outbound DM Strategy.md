# Outbound DM Strategy

## Sebastian's GTM Strategy
- Source: People commenting "KEYWORDS" on high-ticket sales coaching accounts.
- Execution: Hire a VA to DM each person manually with demo access.
- Targets: 200 DMs per day, 10% conversion target.
- Follow-up Rule: Need at least 4 touch points before calling it a cold lead.
- Tooling: Researching ManyChat for follow-up automation.
