## What Wingman Is (One Sentence)

> **Wingman is a real-time AI copilot that sits invisibly on your sales calls and tells you exactly what to say at the exact right moment.**

---

## The Tagline

> **Free to fly. Pay when you win.**

**Why this works:**
- "Free to fly" — the free tier lets closers run real calls. No trial period, no demo mode. Real calls.
- "Pay when you win" — the iMessage trigger fires at the moment of a close. The conversion ask comes when the value is highest. You pay when you're already winning.
- Speaks directly to a commission-based rep who thinks in terms of winning and losing deals.
- Short enough to stick.

**Secondary taglines (testing):**
- "Your second voice on every call."
- "The closer's edge."
- "AI that speaks only when silence would cost you money."
- "Closes don't happen by accident. Neither does this."

## What Problem It Solves and For Who

**The problem:** High-ticket phone closers know what they're supposed to do on a call. They've been trained. They know the methodology. But in the moment, under pressure, with real money on the line — they rush through discovery, skip the press, cave at "I need to think about it." The gap between knowing and doing is where deals die.

**For who:** Commission-based closers selling high-ticket offers ($3k–$15k) over Zoom or Google Meet on a Mac. Already trained on a closing methodology (NEPQ, Cole Gordon, etc.). Closing at 15–30% and want to push higher.

**Not for:** Beginners, SDRs, enterprise AEs, Windows users, anyone who isn't already closing high-ticket on phone.

## The Emotional Outcome

The closer who uses Wingman for 90 days feels like they have a genie on the call. Not a robot reading from a script. Not a notification machine. A genie that whispers exactly the right thing at exactly the right moment, so rarely that when it speaks, it's always important.

The identity shift: from "rep who sometimes struggles with objections" to "rep who always knows what to do."

## What Resonates vs What Falls Flat

**Resonates with closers:**
- Specific moments: "Right when they said 'I need to think about it,' Wingman told me to ask what they were really thinking about."
- ROI framing: "One close pays for a year."
- Peer credibility: "closers who use this hit [X]% higher close rates"
- The invisible element: "the prospect has no idea it's there"
- Autonomy: "you're still closing — Wingman just catches the moments you would have missed"

**Falls flat:**
- Feature lists ("real-time transcription, AI-powered suggestions, call analytics...")
- Enterprise-speak ("conversation intelligence," "revenue enablement," "sales performance management")
- Anything that implies the rep is being replaced ("AI that closes deals for you")
- Anything that sounds like training ("learn the 5 stages of a closer's call")
- Generic AI hype ("powered by the latest AI to supercharge your sales")

## Copy Principles

1. **Sound like a closer, not a SaaS company.** Closers don't say "leverage," "utilize," or "empower." They say "close," "land," "handle," "push."

2. **Specific beats vague.** "Told me to go silent for 12 seconds after price reveal" beats "helps with price objections."

3. **Third-person proof is stronger than feature claims.** A closer describing a Wingman moment beats any feature bullet point.

4. **The prospect's presence is implied.** Everything is written with "you're on a call, it's live, money is on the table." That tension makes the copy feel real.

5. **Never say "AI" where you could say "Wingman."** AI is a category. Wingman is a character. The character is more trustworthy than the category.

## Landing Page Hierarchy (Working Draft)

1. **Hero:** Headline + 1-sentence subhead + "Get Early Access" button. What it is + who it's for.
2. **The Moment:** Video or GIF of a signal card firing on a call. No explanation needed — show the product.
3. **How It Works:** 3 steps. Simple. No jargon.
4. **Social proof:** First beta closers and their results. Verbatim quotes.
5. **The ROI Math:** "One close pays for a year." Simple table.
6. **System requirements:** Apple Silicon Mac only. Zoom, Google Meet, Teams. Be upfront.
7. **FAQ:** Handle the objections (Is it cheating? Does the prospect know? What if it fires too much?)
8. **CTA:** "Get Early Access" or "Join the Beta."

## FAQ Copy (Pre-Written)

**Is this cheating?**
> Every top closer has a coach in their corner. Wingman is that coach — just invisible. You're still the one on the call. You're still the one building the relationship. Wingman just catches the moments most reps miss.

**Does the prospect know?**
> No. Wingman runs on your Mac, visible only to you. It's a floating overlay on your screen that the prospect can never see. There's no bot in the call, no notification sound, nothing on your face or voice that changes. You see the card — they don't.

**Will it fire constantly and distract me?**
> No. Wingman fires a signal card only when silence would genuinely cost you the deal. There's a 90-second minimum between cards. You'll see roughly 5–8 cards on a 60-minute call — most during objection handling and close. The rest of the call, Wingman is silent.

**What if I don't like the suggestion?**
> Dismiss it. One tap. You're always in control of the call. Wingman is a suggestion, not a command.

**Does it work on Windows?**
> Not yet. Wingman requires a Mac with an Apple Silicon chip (M1, M2, M3) running macOS 13 or later. Windows support is on the roadmap but not yet available.
