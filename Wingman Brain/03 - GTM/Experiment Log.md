## What Counts as an Experiment

An experiment is any intentional change to the product or go-to-market where we're testing an assumption and measuring an outcome. Not every change is an experiment — routine bug fixes aren't. But anything where we're unsure what will happen and care about the outcome is.

Experiments to log here:
- Pricing page copy changes
- Onboarding flow tweaks
- iMessage copy variations
- Affiliate outreach approaches (different messages, different communities)
- Hero headline A/B tests
- CTA button copy changes
- Free tier limits (how many calls before the paywall)
- Email subject lines for beta invites

**Log it before you know the result.** Write the hypothesis first. This prevents rationalization of results after the fact.
