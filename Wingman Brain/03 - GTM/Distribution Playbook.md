# Distribution Playbook

Last updated: 2026-04-02

---

## The Core Distribution Philosophy

Distribution for Wingman is not a growth hack. It's a simple equation: make the product undeniably magical for the right person, then put it in front of more of the right people.

The sequence matters: product proof first, distribution second. Affiliates won't promote something that doesn't work. Word of mouth doesn't spread from mediocre experiences. The iMessage funnel doesn't convert if the product hasn't already delivered value. **Everything below is activated in order — not in parallel.**

---

## Phase 1: Founder's Network Beta (Now)

**Goal:** Get 5–10 real closers using Wingman on real calls. Collect evidence. Learn what works.

**How:** Direct outreach to known closers in Sebastian's network and the Cole Gordon community. Personal invites, not a public launch. No paid acquisition.

**What we're measuring:**
- Does the live pipeline work end-to-end? (Transcript → trigger → signal card)
- Do signal cards fire at the right moments?
- Do reps feel coached or annoyed?
- What's the first thing a rep says after their first session?

**What success looks like:** At least one closer who says "this was exactly the right thing to say at exactly the right moment" without being prompted.

---

## Phase 2: iMessage Conversion Funnel (After Product is Proven)

**The mechanism:** Wingman is free for a limited number of calls. When a free-tier user's call volume approaches the limit, AND Wingman detects signals of a close happening on that call, an automated iMessage fires via Blooio/Project Blue.

**The timing is critical:** Don't trigger at call start. Don't trigger based on call count alone. Trigger when the rep is at the Close stage of a free-tier call — the highest emotional moment in the product experience.

**The message (working draft):**
> "Congrats on the close 🎯 Wingman's been in your corner all day. Ready to unlock unlimited calls? [link]"

The rep just (likely) closed. They're feeling the high. The conversion moment is emotionally right.

**The AI reply handling:** When the rep texts back, an AI (via Blooio) reads the reply and routes:
- "Yes" / "How do I do that?" → Direct link to subscribe
- "How much?" → Price reveal + link
- "Not yet" → Graceful follow-up sequence (not aggressive)
- No reply → No follow-up for 48 hours

**Status:** Not yet built. Blooio/Project Blue integration is the dependency.

## Phase 3: Affiliate Program (Rewardful + Stripe)

**The model:** Inspired by Cal AI's growth playbook. Creators and coaches in the closer space get a unique referral link. Every user who subscribes through their link earns them 20% of the subscription revenue — recurring, for as long as the customer stays subscribed.

**Why 20% recurring (not a one-time bounty):**
- Creates ongoing incentive to keep promoting (vs front-loaded bounties where creators promote once and move on)
- Aligns creator success with customer success (creator earns more if the product retains)
- At $149/month, 20% = $29.80/referred user/month. A creator with 10 active referrals earns ~$300/month ongoing. That's meaningful enough to keep the link in their content.

**Infrastructure:** Rewardful handles tracking (unique links, attribution). Stripe handles the commission payouts (Rewardful integrates directly with Stripe).

**Target affiliates (in priority order):**
1. Sales coaches in the closer space who teach frameworks Wingman supports (Cole Gordon-trained, NEPQ trainers)
2. Closers with online audiences (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok content about high-ticket sales)
3. Owners of info product companies whose closing teams could use Wingman

**Onboarding a new affiliate:**
1. Give them a free Wingman account (they need to experience it to promote it authentically)
2. Give them a unique Rewardful link
3. Give them a short demo video they can share
4. Let their own experience be their pitch — don't give them a script

**Status:** Planned. Not yet active. Rewardful account needs to be set up.

## Phase 4: Community Distribution

**Cole Gordon / Closers.io:** The primary community. Alumni are pre-qualified — they already understand the methodology Wingman is built on. Outreach here should feel peer-to-peer, not advertising.

**Facebook and Discord:** The closer community lives in private Facebook groups and Discord servers. Direct messages from a peer who uses Wingman are 10x more effective than ads in these spaces.

**LinkedIn:** Secondary. Closers in the info product space aren't as active here as B2B AEs. But content about close rates and sales performance does circulate.

**YouTube / TikTok:** Longer-term organic play. Content showing Wingman in action on a real call (with a prospect's consent and voice altered/removed) could be powerful. "Watch AI catch my objection in real-time" is inherently viral.

---

## What We Are NOT Doing (Yet)

- **Paid ads:** Too early. Product isn't proven. Ads accelerate what's already working — they don't create it.
- **Cold outreach at scale:** Spray and pray. Not aligned with "put it in front of the right people."
- **Product Hunt launch:** Closer community doesn't live on Product Hunt. This is a vanity launch for a product that needs a community launch.
- **Press:** Premature. No one writes about pre-revenue B2B tools without proof of traction.

## The Viral Mechanism (When It Works)

The natural word-of-mouth loop for Wingman:
1. Closer uses Wingman, closes a deal they might have lost
2. They tell another closer about it ("I had this moment where...")
3. That closer wants to try it
4. They close a deal they might have lost
5. Loop continues

The signal card story is inherently shareable because it's specific and dramatic. "Wingman told me to go silent after price reveal and I just sat there for 12 seconds and the prospect said yes" — that story gets told. It has a moment, a tension, and a resolution. That's word of mouth.
