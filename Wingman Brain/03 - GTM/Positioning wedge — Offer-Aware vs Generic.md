# Positioning Wedge — Offer-Aware vs Generic

**Captured 2026-07-11 (Sebastian, after seeing the Phase 1b live demo on his real offer). Priority: HIGH —
Sebastian: "take note of that big time."**

## The insight
The single clearest way to show why Wingman beats every other real-time sales-copilot tool is a
**side-by-side of the SAME live moment**:
- **Generic card** = what every competitor produces (they don't know your offer).
- **Offer-aware card** = what Wingman produces (it knows your offer's root driver and speaks in your voice).

This isn't a feature bullet — it's the whole **moat, made visible in one screenshot.** Competitors give
generic coaching; Wingman gives a question aimed at *your* offer's root, in *your* language, without
fabricating. That difference is the reason to switch, and most prospects won't *believe* it until they *see*
it.

## Proof point already in hand (this session)
Real offer: land investing. Prospect says *"I'm just tired of my job. I want out."*
- **Generic:** "When you say you want out — what does that actually look like for you?"
- **Offer-aware (Wingman):** "...what would you need to be **making** to pull the trigger on leaving?"
  → chunks the vague goal straight to the cash-flow number that buys their freedom — the offer's root driver —
  and does NOT invent a dollar figure (the DNA held $2–15k/mo; the card let the prospect say it).

## The asset to build
- A **landing-page section and/or a blog post**: "Generic AI vs. Offer-Aware AI — why a copilot that doesn't
  know your offer makes your reps sound like robots." Show real before/after card pairs across a few offers.
- Could be interactive: enter your offer → watch a generic card vs. a Wingman card on a sample moment (a
  self-selling demo — the prospect *feels* the difference on their own offer, which is exactly what sold
  Sebastian in the terminal).
- Ties to the core product promise (CLAUDE.md rules 10/11): generic = robotic = the thing that kills trust;
  offer-aware = feels like intuition.

## Why it's a wedge, not a feature
Every competitor can claim "real-time AI coaching." None can produce a card that knows the rep's specific
offer and root driver, because none captured it (the Sales DNA moat). The side-by-side makes the invisible
moat visible — and it's the demo that converts.
