# Answer cards with clickable sources (Sebastian, 2026-07-07)

For the **← Answer** arrow (Phase 4b): when Wingman answers a prospect's question from grounded material, the card could carry **clickable sources** — click opens the browser at the source.

Why it's powerful: turns "trust me" into "here's the receipt"; also a credibility/content story ("our AI shows its sources").

Build considerations for when this gets its own session:
- HUD is click-through with hover-capture — a clickable link inside a signal card needs the same hover-capture discipline as the outcome card (Known Issue #35 lesson).
- Opening a browser MID-CALL is an attention risk — maybe click = copy link / open a small preview, or sources only appear on the post-call Flight Log version of the card.
- Sources only exist when the answer came from grounded material (offer fields / future ingested content) — the guarded-LLM fallback has no source by definition, which is itself a nice honesty signal.
