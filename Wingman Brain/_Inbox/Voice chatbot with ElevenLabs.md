# Voice Chatbot with ElevenLabs

Captured: 2026-04-23

## The Idea

Give the Wingman chatbot (Mission Intel / Ask Wingman) the ability to talk out loud via ElevenLabs API. Instead of the rep typing questions and reading answers, they could have a real conversation with Wingman — voice in, voice out.

## Why It Could Be Powerful

- **Coaching feels like coaching.** Reps already talk for a living. Making them type to get coached feels clunky. Voice feels natural.
- **Smoother deep-dives.** Post-call debrief, objection breakdowns, blind spot conversations — all of these feel better spoken than typed.
- **Fills the "blind spots" gap without the dashboard problem.** Killed the blind spots feature on 2026-04-23 because a dashboard telling reps "here's what you suck at" is demoralizing. But a rep voluntarily asking the chatbot "what am I missing across my last 10 calls?" out loud, on their own terms, is a completely different emotional experience. The rep opts in, rather than being told.
- **Differentiator.** No sales AI tool has a voice-native coach yet. Meets reps where they already live (talking).

## When

Not now. Post-beta. This is a V2+ feature.
