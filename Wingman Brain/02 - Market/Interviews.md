## Why We Interview

User interviews are not market research theater. Every interview has a specific question it's designed to answer. Before booking an interview, write the question down. After the interview, write the answer.

The most valuable output is almost never the answer to your question. It's the thing they say that you didn't ask about.

## Running Themes Across Interviews

*[Populate as patterns emerge across multiple interviews]*

Themes to track:
- What is the universal pain? (The thing every closer mentions without being asked)
- What words do they use to describe the problem?
- What have they already tried that didn't work?
- What do they say about the price without being asked?
- What is the first question they ask when shown a demo?

---

## Interview Protocol

1. Start with role and background — don't jump to the product immediately
2. Ask "walk me through what a typical call looks like for you" before showing anything about Wingman
3. Ask "what's the hardest moment on a call for you" — the answer is the positioning
4. Show the demo. Then shut up and let them react. Don't explain. Watch what they look at first.
5. Ask "would this have helped you on a call this week?" — specificity forces real evaluation vs abstract enthusiasm
6. End with "what would make you not buy this?" — surfaces real objections they might not volunteer
