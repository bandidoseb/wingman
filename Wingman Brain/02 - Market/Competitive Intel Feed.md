# Competitive Intel Feed

Last updated: 2026-04-02

A running log of anything notable observed about competitors or the competitive landscape. Format as dated entries. Log anything relevant: feature launches, pricing changes, job postings that signal product direction, content they publish, community mentions.

---

## What to Log Here

- Feature announcements or product updates from any adjacent tool
- Pricing changes (increases or decreases signal market dynamics)
- Job postings from competitors — especially engineering or AI roles (signals where they're investing)
- Any mentions of Gong, Chorus, Fathom, Hyperbound, or similar tools in the closer community
- Closers switching FROM any tool TO something else (or back to nothing)
- Any new entrant that looks like it could become a direct competitor

---

## How to Read This Feed

This is a signal file, not a strategy document. Patterns matter more than individual entries. If you start seeing the same tool mentioned repeatedly by closers as "something I tried," that's a signal about alternatives in the market.
