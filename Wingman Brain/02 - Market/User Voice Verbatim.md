# User Voice Verbatim

Last updated: 2026-04-02

---

## Purpose

This file stores **exact quotes from real users and prospects**. Never paraphrase. Never clean up grammar or word choice. The exact language matters.

**Why verbatim?**
The exact words a closer uses to describe their pain become:
- Our marketing copy (the hook that converts)
- The language in signal cards (tie-down — using their exact words back at them)
- The positioning frame (what problem we're really solving)
- The objection handling in our own sales conversations

A paraphrase loses the emotional weight. "I know I should slow down but I don't" hits differently than "I know the methodology, I just can't execute it when there's real money on the line." Both say the same thing. Only one converts.

## Rules

1. **Verbatim only.** If you're not quoting exactly, don't put it here — summarize it in the User Interview note instead.
2. **Context is required.** A quote without context loses half its value. What prompted this? What stage of the conversation were we in?
3. **Date every entry.** Language evolves. Knowing when something was said helps you track if pain points are shifting.
4. **No editing.** If they said "like" five times in a sentence, keep the "likes." That's how they talk. That's how your copy should talk.
