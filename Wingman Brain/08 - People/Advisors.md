## Advisor Conversation Template

When meeting with a potential advisor:

1. **Context first** — explain Wingman in 2 minutes (use the one-sentence version from Messaging.md)
2. **Ask about their experience** — what have they built/closed/grown that's relevant?
3. **Ask for one specific thing** — don't ask for general "advice." Ask: "Here's my specific problem — what would you do?" People engage more with specific questions than vague asks.
4. **Note any commitments they make** — if they say "I'll introduce you to Cole Gordon's team," note it here immediately and follow up within 48 hours.
5. **Don't over-formalize early** — advisors who are worth having don't need an equity agreement to help on the first conversation. Formalize after you've proven the relationship is real.
