## How the Affiliate Program Works

**Commission:** ~20% of monthly subscription ($29.80/referred user/month)
**Type:** Recurring — earned as long as the referred customer stays subscribed
**Tracking:** Rewardful (unique link per affiliate)
**Payout:** Monthly via Rewardful + Stripe

**At 10 active referrals:** ~$298/month to the affiliate. Ongoing.
**At 50 active referrals:** ~$1,490/month to the affiliate. This is meaningful passive income for a creator.

## Affiliate Onboarding Checklist

When bringing on a new affiliate:

- [ ] Create their Rewardful account and unique link
- [ ] Give them a free Wingman account (they must experience the product to promote it authentically)
- [ ] Send them a short demo video they can use in their content
- [ ] Add them to this table
- [ ] Set up Stripe commission tracking through Rewardful
- [ ] Schedule a follow-up 30 days in to check on their promotion and answer questions

---

## Target Affiliate Profiles (Priority Order)

1. **Sales coaches in the closer space** — trainers who teach NEPQ, Cole Gordon methodology, or similar. Their audience is pre-qualified (already interested in tools that improve close rates).

2. **High-ticket closers with online audiences** — reps who post YouTube/TikTok/Instagram content about sales. Their audience trusts their tool recommendations because they actually close for a living.

3. **Info product owners whose teams use Wingman** — if an offer owner's closing team is on Wingman, the owner becomes a natural affiliate for their extended network.

---

## Notes on Affiliate Activation

Don't expect affiliates to promote something they haven't used. The onboarding free account isn't optional — it's the whole point. A creator who has had their own "Wingman moment" on a real call can tell that story authentically. A creator who's just seen a demo will produce generic promotional content that doesn't convert.

**The affiliate's own experience IS the pitch.**
