Last updated: 2026-04-02

## The Math That Matters

At $149/month:
- Every user who stays for 12 months = $1,788 LTV (minimum)
- Churn at 5%/month = avg customer stays 20 months = $2,980 LTV
- Churn at 3%/month = avg customer stays 33 months = $4,917 LTV

**Retention is the multiplier.** Acquiring a user at 5% monthly churn is worth 40% less than acquiring a user at 3% churn. Every improvement in product quality compounds through LTV.
