- **Step 1 — Fixed the eval "measuring stick."** Discovered (via the streaming plan-review) that the objection eval graded a **duplicated copy** of the prompt, not the real one the app uses, and fed Sonnet the objection on a silver platter (production makes Sonnet *infer* it). Created `src/lib/wingman/objection-prompt.mjs` as the **single source of truth** (generated byte-identical from the shipped string, round-trip verified, 7,869 chars), imported by BOTH `objection-signal.ts` and the eval. Made the eval production-faithful (objection inferred, 150-token cap). **Data win:** HINT (objection handed in) vs INFER (inferred) → INFER 19.67 ≥ HINT 19.47, right-belief perfect — **proved removing Groq costs nothing.**

- **Step 2 — Removed Groq from the live objection slot; folded the honesty gate into Sonnet.** One Sonnet call now infers the objection and returns `line` / `abstain` / `error`. Abstain (`{"line": null}`) → the static **"No objection detected"** card (zero extra paid calls, never a guess); error → retry pill. Saves the ~0.3–0.5s sequential Groq hop. `objection-signal.ts` switched from a nullable that conflated abstain with failure to a clean 3-state discriminated union; `kb-signal/route.ts` dropped the distiller branch. **Eval gate PASS:** 7/7 abstains, 0/30 false-silence, tonality 4.93, 0 fabrications. **wingman-reviewer: APPROVE (0 bugs).** Shipped to **PR #23** (pushed, **pinned — not merged**; merges at the real wrap after streaming). `distillObjection` + `generateNudgeCard` now orphaned → Phase 5 #37.

- **Step 3 — Built the smooth HUD reveal loop.** A `requestAnimationFrame` drain loop paints the objection line at a constant rate (proportional ease + linear floor), decoupling bursty arrival from a glassy reveal. Built/tuned against the **current one-shot card** first (already an improvement) — it's the seam where true streaming (Step 4) feeds progressive text. Verified via frame-sim (one-shot 142ch→750ms; bursty streaming ≤4 chars/frame, smooth + terminates). Tunables in Beta Tuning Parameters.md §8. Committed on `feat/streaming-card-reveal`.
