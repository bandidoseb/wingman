## Decisions Made and Why

| Decision | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| Two separate KB tables (`kb_entries` for signal cards, `kb_coaching_entries` for chatbot) | Signal card entries need surgical precision — 30+ first-person triggers, verbatim script, universal applicability. Coaching chatbot needs broader content — mindset, habits, frameworks, scheduling. Same schema can't serve both well. Forced schema = nullable columns everywhere and confused quality bar. |
| Everything ingested goes into kb_coaching, only surgical entries ALSO go into kb_entries | Signal is a subset of coaching. The 215 entries failing signal card bar are still valid coaching content — they shouldn't be deleted, just excluded from live call signal generation. |
| Two separate Sonnet extraction passes (not one combined pass) | Quality over simplicity. A focused coaching extraction prompt produces better coaching entries; a focused signal extraction prompt produces better signal entries. One combined prompt creates compromises in both. |
| Gap-detection signal cards ("you haven't touched on timeline") are Playbook cards, not KB cards | KB only fires when a prospect says something specific. Gap detection is powered by the Playbook framework (pre-call checklist). These are separate systems and should not be confused. |
| cleanup.js --apply deferred until after kb_coaching_entries backfill | The 215 entries should be deleted from kb_entries (not marked low), but only after they're safely copied to kb_coaching_entries. Deleting before backfill = data loss. |
