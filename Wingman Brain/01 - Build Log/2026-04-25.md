Comparing Cluely's subprocessors to Wingman's stack surfaced several actionable gaps:

- Cluely tracks feature usage, drop-off, churn signals via PostHog
- Wingman is flying completely blind — no data on whether signal cards fire, get dismissed, or which stages cause problems
- Free at beta scale
- Decision: add PostHog — dedicated session needed

**Pusher (WebSocket push) — Wingman uses polling**
- Wingman polls every 8–90 seconds depending on call stage (see `src/lib/wingman/poll-intervals.ts`)
- Cluely pushes signal cards the moment they're ready via WebSocket
- Decision: switch to Pusher — full dedicated session needed (see economics below)

**Pinecone — Wingman uses pgvector inside Supabase**
- At 419 KB entries, pgvector is fine
- At scale (tens of thousands of entries), Pinecone is purpose-built and faster
- Decision: migrate to Pinecone proactively — dedicated session needed

**Vercel — Wingman uses Railway**
- Vercel is the native home for Next.js (built by the same team)
- Benefits: better cold starts, edge functions, per-branch preview deploys
- Railway is fine now but Vercel is the right long-term move
- Decision: migrate — not urgent, but add to roadmap

### 3. Polling vs. WebSocket Push — Cost Analysis

Dug into the actual dollar cost of Wingman's current polling architecture vs. WebSocket push.

**Current polling — actual poll count for a 60-minute call:**
- Rapport (90s interval): ~2 polls
- Discovery (30s interval): ~16 polls
- Press (15s interval): ~48 polls
- Confirm (15s interval): ~16 polls
- Pitch (25s interval): ~19 polls
- Close (8s interval): ~90 polls
- **Total: ~191 Sonnet polls per call**

**Hidden cost: Background Haiku KB scan fires every 10 seconds = ~252 Haiku calls per call**

This was the biggest surprise. The background KB scan in `useWingmanPoller.ts` fires every 10 seconds regardless of whether anything meaningful happened. That's $0.41/call in pure waste.

**Per-call AI cost breakdown (60-minute call, prompt caching active):**
| | Current Polling | WebSocket Push |
|---|---|---|
| Sonnet stage tracking | $1.54 | $0.32 |
| Haiku KB scans | $0.41 | $0.02 |
| Post-call (fixed) | $0.03 | $0.03 |
| **Total** | **$1.98** | **$0.37** |

**Savings: 81% reduction in AI inference cost per call.**

Quick win identified: eliminate background Haiku KB scan immediately (30-minute code change, no architecture change needed). Only run KB on keyword triggers. Saves ~$0.41/call right now, before the full WebSocket push migration.

Full WebSocket push migration: estimated 1 day of work. Pusher free tier covers beta scale (1,000 concurrent connections, 200K messages/day). First paid tier: $49/month — negligible against savings.

### 5. Unit Economics Modeling

Built a live margin calculator at `margin-calculator.html` (project root). Interactive sliders for price, calls, call length, and Recall rate. Shows per-user cost breakdown, gross margin, at-scale profit table, and Recall.ai negotiation targets.

**Customer profile established:**
- High-ticket closers working for other people's offers
- Earn ~10% commission
- Volume: ~4–5 scheduled calls/day, 5–6 days/week = ~100–130 calls/month
- **50-call plan is immediately off the table** — they'd hit that in 11 days

**Unit economics at target pricing (Launch plan + WebSocket push):**

Assumptions: 65 calls/month average, 45-minute average call length

| | Cost |
|---|---|
| Recall.ai ($0.0075/min × 65 calls × 45 min) | $21.94 |
| Anthropic (push, scaled to 45 min) | $21.00 |
| Fixed (post-call + hosting + Pusher) | $5.00 |
| **Total cost/user** | **~$48** |

| Price | Margin |
|---|---|
| $149 | 67.8% |
| $179 | 73.2% |
| $199 | 75.9% |

**Worst case user (5 calls/day × 6 days × 60 min):** 130 calls/month
- Cost at Launch plan + push: $111.60/user
- Margin at $179: 37.6%
- Margin at $199: 44.2%

**Direction on pricing:** Leaning toward $179–$199/month, unlimited calls. No per-call cap — high-volume closers (the target user) resent caps and churn when they hit them.

**Critical caveat:** All margin numbers are model assumptions. No real user data yet. Average call length, calls per month, and even Recall.ai Launch plan overage rates are unknown. Revisit with first 10 beta users' real usage data.
