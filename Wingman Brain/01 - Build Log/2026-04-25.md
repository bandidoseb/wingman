### 3. Polling vs. WebSocket Push — Cost Analysis

Dug into the actual dollar cost of Wingman's current polling architecture vs. WebSocket push.

**Current polling — actual poll count for a 60-minute call:**
- Rapport (90s interval): ~2 polls
- Discovery (30s interval): ~16 polls
- Press (15s interval): ~48 polls
- Confirm (15s interval): ~16 polls
- Pitch (25s interval): ~19 polls
- Close (8s interval): ~90 polls
- **Total: ~191 Sonnet polls per call**

**Hidden cost: Background Haiku KB scan fires every 10 seconds = ~252 Haiku calls per call**

This was the biggest surprise. The background KB scan in `useWingmanPoller.ts` fires every 10 seconds regardless of whether anything meaningful happened. That's $0.41/call in pure waste.

**Per-call AI cost breakdown (60-minute call, prompt caching active):**
| | Current Polling | WebSocket Push |
|---|---|---|
| Sonnet stage tracking | $1.54 | $0.32 |
| Haiku KB scans | $0.41 | $0.02 |
| Post-call (fixed) | $0.03 | $0.03 |
| **Total** | **$1.98** | **$0.37** |

**Savings: 81% reduction in AI inference cost per call.**

Quick win identified: eliminate background Haiku KB scan immediately (30-minute code change, no architecture change needed). Only run KB on keyword triggers. Saves ~$0.41/call right now, before the full WebSocket push migration.

Full WebSocket push migration: estimated 1 day of work. Pusher free tier covers beta scale (1,000 concurrent connections, 200K messages/day). First paid tier: $49/month — negligible against savings.
