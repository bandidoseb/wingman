## Goal
Kill the AI tics in the BASE objection card voice — the tics Sebastian (final tonality judge) found and
the AI judge scored 4.8/5 without feeling. Voice before dial (the dial multiplies the base voice).

## What we decided (the smart-approach conversation)
- Read ChatGPT's "conversation architecture" research suggestion. **Rejected its centerpiece** (generate
  → rewrite → score with an LLM humanness metric → iterate): that's the exact loop that already failed us
  (4.8/5, couldn't feel tics). An LLM humanness metric optimizes toward the machine's blind spot. **The
  reward signal must be Sebastian, not a metric** — this is RLHF in miniature: golden exemplars + human
  reward + a deterministic filter for the always-wrong mechanical tics.
- **Root-cause finding (the key one):** the tics are COMMANDED by the DOCTRINE, not the few-shot examples.
  `objection-prompt.mjs:19` literally instructed the permission-ask ("ASK PERMISSION: can I share a
  perspective? / can I make a suggestion? / can I be honest…"); the challenger modifier handed it "come
  on". The base examples were already clean. So a mere example-swap would NOT have worked.
- **Voice philosophy (Sebastian):** the card is a NUDGE, not a script — a good closer adds their own
  voice. Prefer directionally-right + SHORT over "perfectly worded" (a too-perfect line stumps reps into
  robotic recitation → Core Rules #10/#11).
- **Permission-ask ruling:** rip the literal phrasing but KEEP "disarm before you challenge" as a concept,
  in the rep's OWN varied words — never standardize on one phrase (that's how "can I share a perspective"
  becomes the next industry tic). Rejected a live mini-HUD menu of options (violates one-card/intuition
  rules); the fix is natural VARIATION baked into the card + trust the rep to make it theirs. Menu could be
  a V2 "give me another way" affordance, not the live path.
- Docs written: `tasks/card-voice-spec.md` (bar + gold rewrites + root cause + rulings),
  `tasks/card-voice-gameplan.md` (the process: roles, loop, phases, coverage checklist).
