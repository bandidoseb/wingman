### 2. Research — Signal Card Placement & Design
Ran two deep research passes (8 domains of cognitive science and UX research). Key findings:

- **On-call attention** (Stothart et al., 2015) — brain monitors empty space; ambient dot fixes this
- **Orienting reflex** (Franconeri & Simons, 2003) — slide animation triggers involuntary eye-pull; fade suppresses it
- **Dual-task verbal interference** (Pashler, 1994) — ~8 words max processable while speaking under stress → ≤5 word headline rule
- **Gaze aversion** (Kendon, 1967) — natural downward-left thinking gaze → bottom-left is socially invisible
- **Peripheral font size** (Arditi & Cho, 2005) — 18px minimum at 15-20 degrees eccentricity
- **Adaptation timeline** (Schneider & Shiffrin, 1977) — automatic peripheral monitoring develops after ~5-10 calls

## Design Decisions Locked

| Decision | Choice | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Position | Bottom-left | Kendon (1967), Z-pattern, EBU R37 |
| Entry animation | Fade in place, 200ms ease-in | Franconeri & Simons (2003), FAA HUD standard |
| Exit animation | Fade out, 150ms ease-out | Yantis & Jonides (1984) |
| Background | rgba(18,18,24,0.82) + blur | Bauer & Cavonius (1980) |
| Headline size | 18px, weight 700 | Arditi & Cho (2005) |
| Headline limit | ≤5 words | Pashler (1994), Arnsten (2009) |
| Color coding | None — unified dark | Treisman Feature Integration Theory |
| Ambient dot | 8px, 3s pulse, no text | Stothart et al. (2015) |
| Card format early stages | Headline only ≤4 words | Dual-task research |
| Card format objections/close | Headline + italic support sentence | Wickens Multiple Resource Theory (2002) |

5. Tap "got it" — confirm card fades out (not instant disappear)
6. Check objection card shows headline + italic support text below divider
7. Check discovery/nudge card shows headline only (no support text)
