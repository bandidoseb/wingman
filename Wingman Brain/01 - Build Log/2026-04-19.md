## Key Decisions Made

- **Snapshot at session start, not poll time** — zero extra DB queries on poll hot path. Flight plan fetched once in parallel at call creation, frozen into the call record.
- **3s timeout on flight plan fetch** — a slow Supabase query never delays call start. `Promise.race` + async IIFE (not `.catch()`, which doesn't exist on PromiseLike).
- **Independent write paths** — `pillar_coverage` written in a separate try/catch from debrief. Either can fail without blocking the other.
- **No backfill on old calls** — pillar_coverage only appears on calls going forward. Not worth re-running Sonnet on historical data.
- **"Service is busy" (529)** — clarified to Sebastian: Anthropic infrastructure error, not our code. Retry logic (already built) handles it silently.
