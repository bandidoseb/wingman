## Decisions Made and Why

| Decision | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| Tune the SUMMARIZER before continuing to judge dig/objection outputs | Sebastian: the belief-ladder summary is the FOUNDATION every card reads — grading outputs on a shaky foundation is backwards. Do the foundation first. |
| Bake full production-scorecard context into the grind fixtures | A context-free card can't be graded AND it fabricates offer features it can't know. The test must show exactly what the live card sees. |
| Favor question-shaped objection moves (NEPQ) | A statement lets the prospect stay passive; a question makes them resolve the objection themselves (self-persuasion). Jeremy Miner's core. |
| Keep the 4 belief states; relabel ⚠️ "at-risk" → "objected" (queued) | The 4 states drive dig-vs-handle (operationally necessary), but since unlocked = latent objection, "at-risk" reads predictive when it's reactive. (Deferred to the summarizer session — gated prompt.) |
| Payment-terms configurator lives in Sales DNA | Wingman must never float a payment plan to a rep who doesn't offer one (and must know the real terms). First "Sales DNA configurator" — a captured answer rewires the objection prompt. |
| Do NOT drive-by edit the belief-ladder summarizer prompt | It's the chokepoint every card reads, behind a hard eval gate. Label/grading changes ride the summarizer session with `test-belief-ladder.mjs`. |
