## What Broke

Nothing shipped broke. The stress-test on the Bug #30 fix found the fix itself broke two features, caught before push:

- **"Ask Wingman" chatbot would've returned "no transcript available" for every post-processed call** — `src/app/api/ai/chat/route.ts:125` loads the full transcript into Sonnet's context to answer questions about a specific call.
- **Flight Log transcript view would've rendered empty** — `src/components/dashboard/FlightLogTab.tsx:465` reads `call.call_transcript` for display.
- Also referenced by `src/app/api/calls/route.ts:16` and `src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/page.tsx:27`.

Revert shipped. Retention policy moved under Issue #33.

## Decisions Made and Why

| Decision | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| **Kill custom objection handles** (V2 Offer Brain feature) | Text transcripts can't see tonality, authority, or pacing — attribution is broken in both directions (success AND failure). Any stored strategic angle is either too generic to add value over the KB or too prospect-specific to be reusable. Adversarial agent + Sebastian's own critique landed the same conclusion independently. |
| **Kill rep blind spots** (V2 Offer Brain feature) | Adversarial agent brutally catalogued 12+ failure modes: Sonnet hallucinates patterns on demand, the 10-call window is logically incoherent (can't track both improvement and relapse), live-context injection violates Product Rule #4 (fires cards when not needed), and the dashboard framing is confidence-poison for top closers. If a rep wants cross-call coaching, the existing "Ask Wingman" chatbot is the right venue — rep opts in, rep controls framing. |
| **Keep transcripts indefinitely, user-deletable** | 1-year auto-delete looked reasonable but Sebastian's own usage pattern (referring to 6+-month-old transcripts) and industry norm (Gong/Chorus keep until user deletes) made indefinite retention the cleaner answer. The real risk is breach exposure, not retention length — addressed via user-controlled deletion (per-call + account wipe) in Issue #33. |
| **Wiretap compliance is now the #1 pre-beta blocker** (Issue #33) | 2025 case law (*Ambriz v. Google*, *Brewer v. Otter.ai*) confirmed AI transcription IS subject to CIPA and ECPA. Every major competitor uses bot-in-call with visible presence — Wingman + Cluely are the outliers. Shipping a public beta without at least auto-posted chat disclosure + ToS + onboarding acknowledgment is a real litigation risk, not theoretical. |
| **Marketing honesty stance** (principle, saved to GTM) | Preempt industry scrutiny by telling the truth: Wingman helps with the words-and-moments part of sales, but tonality + authority + pacing are still on the rep. Top closers see through overselling. Honesty is a differentiator in this market. |
