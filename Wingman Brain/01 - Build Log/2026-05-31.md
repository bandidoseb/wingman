## Decisions Made and Why

| Decision | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| Signal cards become hotkey-summoned, not autonomous | Autonomous pop-ups distract reps and assume Wingman knows better than the rep when help is needed (it doesn't). Rep knows *when* they're stuck; Wingman supplies *what to say*. Also slashes live AI cost. |
| Keyword classifier demoted to silent scout (tags, never fires) | Keeps fast/free detection, removes the autonomous-firing distraction. Bonus: false positives become harmless, so detection can be made broad/aggressive with zero risk. |
| Menu always visually identical, no greying | Greyed options read as "unavailable"; a changing menu breaks eyes-closed muscle memory. Intelligence lives in the backend, not the menu's look. |
| Visible stage bar removed → ambient presence dot; live stage tracking killed | Peripheral motion/text is the same distraction problem as pop-ups. The recent transcript already reveals stage to the model, so paying to label it live is wasted. Stage analysis moves to post-call debrief. |
| Generate-on-pick first (Option A) | Cheapest, simplest. Measure real latency before adding speculative pre-generation. Don't pay tokens to pre-solve an unconfirmed speed problem. |
| Offer Brain → Sales DNA | More neutral/personal name — "this is *you*, learned," not a feature name. |
| 10 free calls → $149/mo, unlimited, no cap | Free period is a taste, not a Sales-DNA quality gate. Caps are a worse UX than paying; price (or future higher tier) is the margin lever, not caps. Hotkey pivot's near-zero live AI cost makes unlimited affordable (~67% margin worst-case at $149). |
